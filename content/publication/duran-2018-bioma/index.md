abstract: The amount of data available in time series is recently increasing in an
  exponential way, making difficult time series preprocessing and analysis. This paper
  adapts different methods for time series representation, which are based on time
  series segmentation. Specifically, we consider a particle swarm optimization algorithm
  (PSO) and its barebones exploitation version (BBePSO). Moreover, a new variant of
  the BBePSO algorithm is proposed, which takes into account the ositions of the particles
  throughout the generations, where those close in time are given more importance.
  This methodology is referred to as weighted BBePSO (WBBePSO). The solutions obtained
  by all the algorithms are finally hybridised with a local search algorithm, combining
  simple segmentation strategies (Top-Down and Bottom-Up). WBBePSO is tested in 13
  time series and compared against the rest of algorithms, showing that it leads to
  the best results and obtains consistent representations.
